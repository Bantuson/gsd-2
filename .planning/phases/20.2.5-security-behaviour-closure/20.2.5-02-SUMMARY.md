- **Found during:** Task 2 (Rewrite security-tauth.test.ts)
- **Issue:** ws.close() in onopen fired onclose synchronously, resolving the promise before reject() was called (settled flag didn't protect against synchronous race)
- **Fix:** Call done(error) BEFORE ws.close() so reject fires before onclose can settle the promise
- **Files modified:** packages/mission-control/tests/security-tauth.test.ts
- **Verification:** B51 now correctly fails RED (WS accepts without auth token)
